    Dear Amanda, my queen,

    Today the world gets to celebrate the day you arrived, and I get to celebrate that you chose to share your days with me.

    You make everything softer. Hard weeks feel lighter after one conversation with you, and good days feel complete only when you are in them. You never have to try to be wonderful. You simply are.

    I wish I could be there to hold you today. Since I cannot, I made this instead, so a small piece of me is with you. Distance is only a line on a map. It has never changed how close you are to my heart.

    I wish you a year full of gentle mornings, big laughter, and every dream you have been quietly carrying. Whatever it brings, I will be cheering the loudest, from wherever I am.

    Thank you for being you. I love you more than this page could ever hold.

    Forever yours

    You are loved today and every day, more than you know, no matter how many miles are between us.
